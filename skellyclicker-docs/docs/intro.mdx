SkellyClicker is a FreeMoCap desktop application for working with keypoint labels in recorded camera images. The current ZED stereo workflow uses an STL reference. Support for opening general FreeMoCap recordings is in development.

## Recordings and projects

A **recording** is one capture containing synchronized camera videos. A **camera view** shows a moment in the recording from one camera’s perspective. **Calibration** relates the camera views to a shared 3D coordinate system.

A **project** is where SkellyClicker saves work associated with a recording, such as image selections and processing results.

## Targets and keypoints

A **target** is the body being tracked. Its **keypoints** are named locations that you want to identify across images. A target can be rigid or non-rigid. A rigid target may also have an STL mesh describing its surface.
